Fine-tune each field

For cheque-only printing, add a custom paper size of 92 × 202 mm (width × length) in the printer settings and feed the cheque long edge first. If the printer still feeds blank paper before the data, try "turned left" or the A4 option with the cheque at the other end. Test first on a plain paper cut to the same size. If the text lands upside down or on the wrong end, switch between the two cheque-only options. The test sheet prints dashed guides and sample text. Lay your cheque on it, or hold both to the light, then adjust the shifts until every guide sits on its line or box. Settings are remembered on this device.
