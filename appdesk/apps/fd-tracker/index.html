EIR (Effective Interest Rate) — the annual yield of the deposit once its compounding frequency is applied: (1 + r/n)ⁿ − 1. Not applicable when interest is paid out rather than reinvested. ARR (Annualised Rate of Return) — the return actually accrued so far, at maturity, or on premature break, annualised from the real elapsed days: (Value ÷ Principal)^(365/days) − 1. Matured, broken and settled deposits are struck through and listed last. Tick rows to export just those; leave none ticked to export whatever the filters above are currently showing.
